About project

:)

BikeToolShare is a community-driven platform created to connect cyclists who want to share, lend, or request tools for bicycle repair and maintenance. Whether you're missing a specific wrench, need a chain tool for a quick fix, or simply want to offer your equipment to others nearby — this is the space to do it.

Our mission is to promote sustainability, collaboration, and self-sufficiency among cycling enthusiasts. Instead of purchasing tools that are rarely used, users can borrow from others in their neighborhood or offer their own tools for temporary use. It’s not just about saving money — it’s about building trust and community.

Users can browse a catalog of tools, filter them by category, view detailed descriptions and images, and request to borrow items directly from other members. Every user has a profile with location-based information to simplify meeting up and sharing.

We believe that cycling is not only a means of transport, but a lifestyle — and maintaining your bike shouldn't be a barrier. With BikeToolShare, we hope to make bike maintenance more accessible and social for everyone.
